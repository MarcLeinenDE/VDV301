**Active disproof:** Could `InstallationSuccessfull` be a second valid status or a different normative version? No: the actual enum table of **each** edition and each selected exact XSD enumerates the one-`l` `InstallationSuccessful`; the XSD repeated typo is contained inside `xs:documentation` of an `UpdateStatus` member, not an `xs:enumeration value`. Exact-instance tests reject the two-`l` spelling in four selected XSD variants. Normal English spelling and the sibling `InstallationFailed` are supporting local spelling attribution, **not substitutes for the executable declaration**. A mistaken type, a generic alias, or a choice/legend convention does not explain the extra `l`.
